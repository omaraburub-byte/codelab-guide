Get a free Gemini API key

CodeLab explains your code with your own AI key. Here is how to get one from Google for free and connect it in Visual Studio.

About 2 minutesFree, no credit cardJust a Google account
Step 1

Open Google AI Studio

Go to aistudio.google.com and sign in with your Google account if it asks. Then click the key icon at the bottom left.

Shortcut: open aistudio.google.com/apikey directly. The first time, Google may ask you to accept its terms.

Step 2

Click Create API key

You are now on the API Keys page. The button is at the top right.

Step 3

Keep the defaults and click Create key

The name and project are already filled in. You don't need to change anything.

Step 4

Click Copy key

Your key is now on the clipboard.

Treat the key like a password. Don't post it online or share it. If it leaks, delete it on the same page and create a new one.
Step 5

Paste it into CodeLab

In Visual Studio open View › Other Windows › CodeLab, then the settings icon. Make sure Gemini is selected, paste the key and click Save.

CodeLab tests the key right away and shows Key works. The key is stored in Windows Credential Manager on your PC only.

The free tier has a daily limit. If CodeLab ever shows error 429, wait a little or switch to a lighter model in settings. On the free tier, Google may use prompts to improve its products.
